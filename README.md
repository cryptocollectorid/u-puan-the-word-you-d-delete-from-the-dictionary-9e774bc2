# The Word You'd Delete From the Dictionary

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-the-word-you-d-delete-from-the-dictionary-9e774bc2): name the one word you would erase from the dictionary forever.

## The word

**defenestration** — the act of throwing someone out of a window.

A real word every reader recognises, funny at first sight because of its absurd specificity, and the humour comes from the word itself: its clunky sound, and the total gap it would leave behind — no other word names that exact act.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single word to `/output/entry.txt`, exactly as the program harness requires (one word, nothing added around it).

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
